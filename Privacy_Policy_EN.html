7.4. RIGHT TO ERASURE

You have the right to request the deletion of Data about you by deleting your Account using the "Delete Account" function in the Game settings.

As a result of deleting your Account, you will lose access to the Game, including any game progress you have earned.

Personal data is destroyed within a period not exceeding 30 days:

  • from the date the processing purpose is achieved;
  • from the date a withdrawal of consent is received (in the absence of other legal grounds for processing);
  • from the date an account deletion request is confirmed.

The recovery period for a deleted account provided for by the Appendix is included in the specified thirty-day period. Longer retention periods apply exclusively to data that the Operator is required to retain under applicable law.

Recovery period. You can restore your Account within the established recovery period of 14 days from the date the deletion request is submitted. During this period, your data will be blocked and not subject to processing. This feature helps you avoid accidentally losing various Game-related information and other important data. During the recovery period, you can complete financial and other transactions that you may have started before submitting the Account deletion request. The process of deleting Your data may take up to one month from the end of the specified recovery period and may be extended by two months depending on the complexity and number of requests.

Retained information. After the recovery period expires, we will delete all Your data, except for data necessary to comply with applicable law (for example, on taxation and accounting), and to detect, investigate and prevent violations of the Game rules or the User Agreement, or to resolve disputes. Information you previously published in our Products remains visible after your Account is deleted. We also do not control data copied by other users of our Products.
