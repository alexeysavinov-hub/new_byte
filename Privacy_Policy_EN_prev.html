You can opt out of personalized advertising in our Games by going to the privacy settings section of your Android or iOS device and selecting "Limit Ad Tracking" (for Apple iOS) or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (for Android).

If the processing of Your data is based on legitimate interests, you may exercise your right to object to such processing at any time. If you object, and in the absence of compelling legitimate grounds for the processing or where the data is not needed for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims, we will stop processing Your data.

You have the right to access Data about you that we have received. Upon your request, we will provide a copy of your personal data in a structured electronic format. If a request affects the rights and freedoms of others or is manifestly unfounded, or in the case of an excessive number of requests, we reserve the right to charge a reasonable fee for fulfilling the request (taking into account the associated administrative costs) or to refuse to fulfill it.

7.3. RIGHT TO RECTIFICATION

You decide for yourself which nickname, email address, payment methods and other account details are associated with your Account. If any of the above data changes, you can make the changes in your own Account. Please let us know if any Data about you is inaccurate by contacting admin@byteplay.fun.

7.4. RIGHT TO ERASURE

You have the right to request the deletion of Data about you by deleting your Account using the "Delete account" function in the Game settings.

As a result of deleting your Account, you will lose access to the Game, including your accumulated game progress.

Recovery period. You can restore your Account within the established recovery period of 14 days from the date of submitting the deletion request. During this period, your data will be blocked and will not be processed. This feature helps you avoid accidentally losing various Game-related information and other important data. During the recovery period, you can complete financial and other transactions that you may have started before submitting the Account deletion request. The process of deleting Your data may take up to one month from the end of the specified recovery period and may be extended by two months depending on the complexity and number of requests.

Retained information. After the recovery period expires, we will delete all of Your data, except for data necessary to comply with applicable law (for example, tax and accounting requirements), and to detect, investigate and prevent violations of the Game rules or the End User License Agreement (EULA), or to resolve disputes. Information that you previously published in our Products remains visible after your Account is deleted. We also do not control data copied by other users of our Products.
